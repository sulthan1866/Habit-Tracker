      <h1 style={{ color: '#333', borderBottom: '2px solid #4CAF50', paddingBottom: '5px' }}>Understanding the Framework</h1>

      <h3>
        <br/>
        <p>
          The Cue-Routine-Reward system breaks down habit formation into three essential elements. By understanding these, you can create habits that stick.
        </p>
        <div style={{ marginLeft: '20px', listStyle: 'disc' }}>
          <strong>Cue:</strong> A trigger or signal that starts the habit.<br/>
          <strong>Routine:</strong> The action you want to establish as a habit.<br/>
          <strong>Reward:</strong> The benefit or satisfaction that reinforces the habit.<br/>
        </div>
      </h3>
      <br/><br/>
      <h1 style={{ color: '#333', borderBottom: '2px solid #4CAF50', paddingBottom: '5px' }}>Steps to Build a Habit</h1>
      <h3>
        <div style={{ marginLeft: '20px', padding: '10px', background: '#f9f9f9', borderRadius: '5px' }}>
          <strong>Identify the Cue:</strong> Choose a consistent trigger to begin your habit.<br/>
          <strong>Define the Routine:</strong> Specify the exact behavior you wish to perform.<br/>
          <strong>Choose a Reward:</strong> Pick a reward that motivates and reinforces the habit.<br/>
          <strong>Repeat Consistently:</strong> Practice daily to establish the habit firmly.<br/>
        </div>
      </h3>
      <br/><br/>
      <h1 style={{ color: '#333', borderBottom: '2px solid #4CAF50', paddingBottom: '5px' }}>Tips for Success</h1>
      <h3>
        <p>
          Start small—build momentum with manageable steps. Be patient; forming habits takes time. If you miss a day, don’t stress; get back on track quickly.
        </p>
        <p style={{ background: '#e8f5e9', padding: '10px', borderRadius: '5px', color: '#2e7d32' }}>
          Remember: Consistency is the key to transforming routines into automatic habits.
        </p>
      </h3>
